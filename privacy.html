Privacy Policy

ClipNest for Mac · Last updated November 9, 2026

What ClipNest collects

Nothing. ClipNest does not collect, upload, sell, or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising code, and no tracking. It doesn't connect to the internet at all.

What stays on your Mac

To let you paste things again, ClipNest saves what you copy: text with its formatting, images, and the locations of files you copy, along with your settings. All of this is saved only on your Mac, inside ClipNest's private storage, and is never sent anywhere.

You decide what's kept. Passwords that password managers mark as secret are never saved. You can leave out copies made in apps you choose, pause recording, delete single items, clear the history, or choose how many copies to keep. Deleting the app removes everything it saved.

Accessibility permission

If you allow it, ClipNest uses the macOS Accessibility permission only to press ⌘V, so that the item you choose is pasted into the app you're using. It doesn't read other apps' windows or what you type.

To leave out the apps you choose, ClipNest checks which app is in front when something is copied. That isn't saved.

Children

ClipNest does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
